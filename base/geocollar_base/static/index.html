Draw as
While the dog stays in a band, the collar repeats its beep or vibration this often. Once the dog is past the line it goes quiet, and stays quiet until it is back inside and clear of every band.
GPS accuracy: the collar's UC6580 receiver is rated 1.5 m (RMS) in open sky. On a dog, near buildings or under trees, expect 2 to 5 m, with the odd jump of 10 m or more. Keep each band at least 3 m wide, and draw the line a few metres inside anything that really matters, such as a road.

    Simulation

    Simulated
    Not a real collar. It checks every position against its fences with the same rules as the collar firmware, so you can try fences before the hardware is ready.
    A real collar reports when its state changes, every 10 minutes, and every 30 s when lost. The simulator reports more often so you can watch it.
