GeoCollar

    Link this phone to a collar

    1. Open the GeoCollar base station on a computer or tablet.
    2. Pick the collar and press Connect app.
    3. Scan the code it shows with the button below.

    A linked phone talks to the collar directly over Bluetooth, away from home too. It can show where the collar is, mute its lost alert, and make it beep or vibrate. It can never shock.
